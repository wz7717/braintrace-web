# Human GEO sources and repository policy

The human fixed models/PLM were project-generated from [GSE46706](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE46706) and [GSE60862](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE60862), not downloaded from GEO as these final model arrays.

Annotation sources include the [GSE46706 supplement directory](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE46nnn/GSE46706/suppl/), its [full adapted NetAffx31 MPS](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE46nnn/GSE46706/suppl/GSE46706_Core_adapted_from_netaffyx31.txt.gz), and [GPL5175](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GPL5175) full build32 annotation. pd design and AHBA static Entrez sources have their own retained scope.

The recorded [NCBI Molecular Data Usage policy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/) does not impose repository-level restrictions on use/distribution of deposited molecular data, while explaining that NCBI cannot transfer intellectual-property rights a submitter may retain. Positive repository scope does not become an NCBI grant for every third-party content item or project-generated file. Public expression records are distinct from separately controlled genotype/dbGaP objects.

Source accessions, original study attribution and notices should be retained without implying endorsement by NCBI, the original investigators or the manufacturer. Accession links identify original resources; they are not substitutes for this exact frozen set or proof that current downloads reproduce its original bytes. Complete raw CEL/matrix/annotation source distributions are not included here. This statement carries forward the recorded source policy; no fresh source download or byte certification is claimed.
