# XSAnno software, 2014 materials and 2017 source scope

Three source objects retain distinct scope:

1. The [original XSAnno README](https://github.com/YingZhuLab/XSAnno/blob/master/README.txt) records GPLv3 for the software. XSAnno software is not included. Referencing its method or annotation source does not by itself label the complete mapping JSON as GPL software, nor waive any genuinely applicable software term.
2. The [2014 article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4035071/) records CC BY 2.0 for the article and CC0 for data supplied by that article unless otherwise stated. Exceptions and third-party scope remain as stated. These original packages are not included.
3. Sousa2017 common-count/common-annotation metadata uses XSAnno common IDs, but the examined records did not authenticate a file-by-file match with a specific 2014 attachment or establish all 2017 file-specific terms. The 2014 CC0 scope is not automatically transferred to 2017 counts, their source annotation or the complete combined mapping. See [PsychENCODE source limits](PSYCHENCODE_SOURCE_LIMITS.md).

The fixed mapping retains its original strict relationships and collision exclusions. It is not regenerated, symbol-rescued or replaced by new annotation. Only licensable project combination contributions are covered by project CC; factual IDs, unprotected statistics, public-domain material and legal exceptions acquire no new restriction. Retain actual XSAnno creator/source attribution without inventing copyright lines or sole authorship. Unknown historical source details do not establish a prohibition or a requirement to rerun the mapping.
