# Bo source, derivatives and NCBI policy

C06 contains the fixed floor_clip Network model/transform. C09 contains the unweighted all819 source raw mean, 43 reference/member pairs and shared axes/indexes. The six additional pairs were built from source, not external PsychENCODE expression or truth. The lower raw mean and Network scaler have separate roles. RULES and the other original records retain member/identity/provenance fields; they are not described as free of Bo-derived metadata.

The following exact original processed files remain excluded:

- mfas5_819samples_28415genes_featurecounts_counts.txt
- mfas5_819samples_23605genes_vsd4_rmbatch.xls
- Information of sequenced samples_update_full878_filter819.xlsx

The existing non-redistribution decision for those files does not automatically prohibit or authorize derived models, raw means or references. The project-contribution grant does not waive any actually applicable original provider condition. Previously examined records did not identify a concrete provision condition explicitly covering this derivative-sharing scope; that does not certify absence of all conditions and does not invent a required new permission.

[Bo2023 article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10023667/) material within its stated scope uses CC BY 4.0, with attribution, licence/change notices and third-party credit-line exceptions. It is not automatically the same object as the separately provided processed files or every BrainTrace derivative.

[PRJNA905082](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA905082) identifies raw RNA-seq/SRA acquisition. The [NCBI Molecular Data Usage policy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/) has positive repository-level use/distribution scope but does not transfer third-party rights. Public raw reads do not supply the byte-identical processed sources or frozen assets.

[Zenodo 7641873](https://zenodo.org/records/7641873) identifies original analysis software v1.0.0, recorded as open with other-open licensing. It is a code record, not a confirmed MIT/CC licence for the three processed files or BrainTrace assets. Original paper, raw-data and software scope are not merged. No original data, software archive or replacement reconstruction is included here.
