# Macaque third-party source and contribution boundaries

[Project contribution terms](PROJECT_CONTRIBUTION_TERMS.txt) apply only to contributions the licensor has authority to license. The 98 mixed files are not uniformly CC or MIT; factual IDs, public-domain material, unprotected statistical values and legal exceptions are not newly restricted.

| Material | Retained source scope |
|---|---|
| C06 fixed Network; C09 raw mean, references, member records and shared support | Bo processed-source derivation and project construction; [Bo/NCBI notice](BO_SOURCE_SCOPE_AND_NCBI_POLICY.md). The original three processed source files are excluded, without inferring either blanket derivative prohibition or blanket permission. |
| PsychENCODE strict combined mapping | Original Ensembl91 Compara one-to-one relations, XSAnno common IDs and retained collision exclusions; [Ensembl](ENSEMBL_DATA_TERMS_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md) and [XSAnno](XSANNO_SOURCE_AND_TERMS.md). |

| Sousa2017 original count/common annotation | Distinct original-source and same-version limitations in [PsychENCODE notice](PSYCHENCODE_SOURCE_LIMITS.md); original complete source files are not included. |
| RULES, BEAMS, REGION_PARENT, SCHEME and original input contract | Preserve original member, identity, attribution and historical metadata. They may contain Bo-derived fields. Referenced code/data are not automatically included or licensed by citation. |

Ensembl's positive own-data scope, actual third-party conditions, XSAnno software GPLv3 and the separate 2014 article/data scopes remain distinct. No new third-party copyright line, sole-authorship statement or provider permission is invented. Existing original notices and actually applicable provider terms retain their scope.
